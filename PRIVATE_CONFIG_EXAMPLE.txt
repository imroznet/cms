Create ahmad-private/config.php OUTSIDE the web root, next to the staging directory:
<?php
putenv('AHMAD_DB_HOST=localhost');
putenv('AHMAD_DB_NAME=YOUR_STAGING_DATABASE');
putenv('AHMAD_DB_USER=YOUR_STAGING_USER');
putenv('AHMAD_DB_PASSWORD=YOUR_NEW_PASSWORD');
putenv('AHMAD_RECAPTCHA_SITE_KEY=');
putenv('AHMAD_RECAPTCHA_SECRET_KEY=');

Do not reuse the database password from the uploaded archive. Restrict the private directory to the hosting account owner.
